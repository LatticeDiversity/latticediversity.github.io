Marianna is a post-doctoral researcher at ETH Zurich. As a woman in physics and
a young mother, she has spent much of her academic career within minority groups
in the scientific community, experiences that motivate her commitment to
building more welcoming and supportive environments. She previously volunteered
as a children’s teacher for a non-profit organization in Africa, an experience
that strengthened her interest in inclusion and community engagement. Through
the LDIC, she hopes to contribute to fostering a positive and accessible
atmosphere at Lattice conferences—including online events—and to help maintain a
transparent, community-supported code of conduct that promotes respectful and
equitable participation for all.
