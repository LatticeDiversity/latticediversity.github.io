Antonin currently occupies a Personal Chair of Theoretical High-Energy Physics at the University of Edinburgh. He held postdoctoral positions at the universities of Southampton and Edinburgh and obtained his PhD in 2012 at the Centre de Physique Théorique in Marseille, France. He has experience recruiting researchers from the PhD level to senior positions and has served on numerous committees of national and international relevance. He actively promoted improving EDI aspects in these contexts and is now extending his action to the lattice community through his service on the LDIC.
