Archana is a post-doctoral researcher at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research in India, with long-standing involvement in diversity, equity, and inclusion efforts throughout her academic career. She has served as a DEI mentor for incoming physics graduate students, helped secure APS funding to establish a Women in STEM chapter, contributed to the DEI Board at William & Mary, and supported female students through the Vigyan Vidushi program at TIFR. Motivated by the need to ensure that all voices—especially those from underrepresented backgrounds—are heard and respected, she hopes to contribute to the LDIC by expanding mentorship opportunities and promoting training on unconscious bias, inclusive communication, and micro-aggressions, helping foster a supportive and equitable environment across the lattice community.
