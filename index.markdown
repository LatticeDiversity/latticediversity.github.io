<div class="spotlight">
<p>The Lattice Diversity and Inclusion Committee (LDIC) is an <a href="https://latticediversity.github.io/about/#current-committee-members">elected
committee</a> charged with improving the intellectual and social climate at the Lattice conference and more broadly within the field of lattice QCD,
responding to issues of harassment and misconduct, and promoting a welcoming atmosphere, diversity and inclusion in our field.</p>
</div>
<div class="spotlight">
<p>We maintain an exemplar <a href="https://latticediversity.github.io/about/#recommended-code-of-conduct">code of conduct</a>, and receive, review
and respond to reports of unprofessional conduct and harassment in the lattice community, according to our
<a href="https://latticediversity.github.io/about/#incident-report-submission-procedure-and-ldic-incident-response-workflow">incident
response procedures</a>. The LDIC also produces <a href="https://latticediversity.github.io/surveys/">surveys and reports</a> on diversity
and inclusion efforts in the field as a whole, periodically presenting these results to the lattice community.</p>
</div>
