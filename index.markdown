<div class="spotlight">
The Lattice Diversity and Inclusion Committee (LDIC) is an [elected committee](https://latticediversity.github.io/about/#current-committee-members)
charged with improving the intellectual and social climate at the Lattice conference and more broadly within the field of lattice QCD,
responding to issues of harassment and misconduct, and promoting a welcoming atmosphere, diversity and inclusion in our field.

We maintain an exemplar [code of conduct](https://latticediversity.github.io/about/#recommended-code-of-conduct), and receive,
review and respond to reports of unprofessional conduct and harassment in the lattice community, according to our
[incident response procedures](https://latticediversity.github.io/about/#incident-report-submission-procedure-and-ldic-incident-response-workflow)
The LDIC also produces [surveys and reports](https://latticediversity.github.io/surveys/) on diversity and inclusion efforts
in the field as a whole, periodically presenting these results to the lattice community.
